The week

  • 4 lifts a week — Upper A, Lower A, Upper B, Lower B. Any 4 days that leave a rest day here and there.
  • Stay active 2–3 days — basketball, a run, a bike, a swim, even a solid walk or hike. Anything that gets him moving counts.
  • Each lift is ~45 min: warm up 5 min, run the 6 moves, done.

The lifts

Upper A · push + core
  1. DB or barbell bench press — 3×8
  2. Lat pulldown — 3×10
  3. DB shoulder press — 3×10
  4. Seated cable row — 3×10
  5. Lateral raise — 3×12
  6. Triceps pushdown — 3×12
Abs / core finisher
  1. Hanging knee raise — 3×12
  2. Cable crunch — 3×15
  3. Plank — 3×30–45s
Lower A · squat
  1. Goblet or back squat — 3×8
  2. Romanian deadlift — 3×10
  3. Lunge or split squat — 3×10/leg
  4. Seated leg curl — 3×12
  5. Calf raise — 3×15
  6. Plank — 3×30s
Upper B · pull
  1. Overhead press — 3×8
  2. Pull-up or assisted — 3×8
  3. Incline DB press — 3×10
  4. One-arm DB row — 3×10
  5. Face pull — 3×15
  6. DB curl — 3×12

  1. Trap-bar deadlift — 3×6
  2. Leg press or Bulgarian split squat — 3×10
  3. Hip thrust — 3×10
  4. Leg extension — 3×12
  5. Calf raise — 3×15
  6. Hanging knee raise — 3 sets

Eating for the goal

  • Slight surplus: weight should trend up ~¼–½ lb a week. 155 → ~170 over a few months.
  • Protein every meal: ~150g/day (roughly 1g per lb). Eggs, chicken, beef, fish, dairy, whey.
  • Carbs around training: rice, oats, potatoes, fruit — fuel and recovery.
  • Sleep 8 hours. It's when the muscle actually gets built.

The 3 rules he can't mess up

  • Add a little weight whenever the last set feels easy.
  • Hit protein and don't skip meals — the scale going up is the win.
  • Show up 4 times. Consistency beats everything else.
