How it works

  • 5 lifts a week, everything trained twice: Push, Pull, Legs+Abs, Upper, Legs+Abs. Two rest days wherever they fit.
  • Overload every week — when he hits the top of a rep range on all sets, add weight next time. The app graphs each lift so you can see it climb.
  • ~45–60 min per session: warm up, 2 heavy compounds, then the isolation work for the pump.

12-week progression

  • Weeks 1–2 — Groove it. Lighter loads, perfect form, leave 3 reps in the tank. Build the habit.
  • Weeks 3–6 — Load it. Add weight or a rep every week. Push close to failure (1–2 reps left) on the last set.
  • Week 7 — Deload. Cut sets ~40%, lighter. Recover and rebound.
  • Weeks 8–11 — Send it. Heaviest block. Add a set to lagging areas (shoulders, arms) if recovering well.
  • Week 12 — Reassess. Photos, measurements, lift numbers vs day 1. Then run it back heavier.
The 3-month truthAs a beginner eating enough, 12 weeks of this = a dramatically different body. Full "visible abs, clearly jacked" is more a 9–18 month arc — abs show up as body fat drops too. Massive change in 3 months, goal physique over the year.

Eat to grow

  • Slight surplus: weight trends up ~¼–½ lb/week. Under-eating is the #1 reason lifting doesn't show.
  • Protein every meal toward the daily target. The meal library is built to make this easy.
  • Carbs around training for fuel; sleep 8 hours — that's when muscle is built.

Supplements worth it

  • Creatine monohydrate, 5g daily — cheapest, most-proven thing for size and strength. Any time of day, every day.
  • Whey protein — not magic, just an easy way to hit protein.
  • Everything else is optional. Food, sleep and consistency do 95% of the work.
