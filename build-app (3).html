How it works

  • 5 lifts a week, everything trained twice: Push, Pull, Legs+Abs, Upper, Legs+Abs. Two rest days wherever they fit.
  • Overload every week — when he hits the top of a rep range on all sets, add weight next time.
  • ~45–60 min per session: warm up, 2 heavy compounds, then isolation work for the pump.

12-week progression

  • Weeks 1–2 — Groove it. Lighter loads, perfect form, leave 3 reps in the tank.
  • Weeks 3–6 — Load it. Add weight or a rep every week. Push to 1–2 reps from failure on the last set.
  • Week 7 — Deload. Cut sets ~40%, lighter. Recover and rebound.
  • Weeks 8–11 — Send it. Heaviest block. Add a set to lagging areas if recovering well.
  • Week 12 — Reassess. Photos, weight, lift numbers vs day 1. Run it back heavier.

Eat to grow

  • Slight surplus: weight trends up ~¼–½ lb/week. Under-eating is the #1 reason lifting doesn't show.
  • Protein every meal toward the daily target. The meal library makes it easy.
  • Carbs around training; sleep 8 hours — that's when muscle is built.

Supplements worth it

  • Creatine monohydrate, 5g daily — cheapest, most-proven thing for size and strength. Any time, every day.
  • Whey protein — just an easy way to hit protein.
  • Everything else is optional. Food, sleep and consistency do 95% of the work.
